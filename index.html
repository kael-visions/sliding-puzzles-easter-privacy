Privacy Policy for Easter Puzzle Adventure

Effective Date: March 2026

Easter Puzzle Adventure is a relaxing offline sliding puzzle game featuring colourful Easter-themed artwork and playful seasonal designs suitable for players of all ages. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collection

Easter Puzzle Adventure does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data. The app functions offline and does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Data Storage

All game progress, unlocked images, puzzle settings, and preferences are stored locally on the user’s device only. No data is uploaded to external servers or cloud services.

Sharing Content

The app may include an optional sharing feature that allows users to share completed puzzle images or the app name using other applications installed on their device. This action is performed entirely on the user’s device. Easter Puzzle Adventure does not track, log, or store any shared content.

Accounts and Authentication

Easter Puzzle Adventure does not require account creation, login, or user authentication.

Notifications

Optional local notifications may be used to enhance gameplay and remind users of seasonal puzzle events. Notification data is processed locally on the device and is not transmitted externally.

In-App Purchases

Easter Puzzle Adventure may offer optional in-app purchases to unlock additional puzzle packs or seasonal content. Payments are processed securely through Google Play Billing. The app does not collect, store, or have access to payment or financial information.

Permissions

Easter Puzzle Adventure only uses permissions required for core gameplay features such as local notifications or sharing images through other apps on the device. No permissions are used to collect personal or sensitive user information.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Play services strictly for in-app billing and essential app distribution functions. These services may process limited technical information as required by Google Play. Easter Puzzle Adventure does not access, collect, or store this information.

Children’s Privacy

Easter Puzzle Adventure is suitable for players of all ages. The app does not collect, store, or transmit personal information from users, including children under the age of 13. Because the app operates offline and does not require accounts or personal information, no personal data is collected from any users.

User Control

Users have full control over their data and may reset game progress or remove the app at any time using standard device controls.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated occasionally to reflect improvements to the app or changes in platform requirements. Any updates will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this page.
